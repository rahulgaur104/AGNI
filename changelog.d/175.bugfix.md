A Zernike mode block of the JD preconditioner that is not positive definite at
the shift is factored at the first of 10, 100 and 1000 times the shift where it
is (``SHIFT_LADDER``), and JD no longer returns NaN because of it. At 48x48x16,
``penalty`` 1e4 and shift 1e-4 the ``(0, 0)`` block has a pivot of 2.5e-8 and
rounds to indefinite. JD keeps its target and every other block is unchanged.
Adds tests of JD against the dense solve on the tokamak and the stellarator at
``penalty`` 1e-2 and 1e4.
