JD on the Zernike basis uses a new preconditioner with one block per Fourier mode
``(m, n)``, over all radii and the three displacement components, applied with an
FFT in theta and zeta. The ring blocks it replaces cannot hold the Zernike
penalty, which couples radii within a mode, and JD on the stellarator took 22 and
55 corrections at ``penalty`` 0.1 and 1 and failed at 1e2 and 1e4. JD now takes 6
to 10 corrections from 0.1 to 1e4 and matches the dense solve. A toroidal family
``x`` is multiplied by ``exp(-i x zeta)`` before the FFT, so the families other
than 0 separate into modes as well.
