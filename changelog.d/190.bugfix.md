``Basis(radial="gauss_radau_jacobi")`` integrated the energy against the Jacobi
weight ``(1 - x)^alpha (1 + x)^beta`` of its nodes instead of the plain ``dx`` of
the paper's Eq. 26: its radial weights summed to 2.79 instead of 0.99 on the
default map, and ``gamma^2`` was wrong at every resolution (DSHAPE, ``n = 2``,
96x96: 1.11e-3 instead of 2.91e-4). The weights are now
``quadrature.interpolatory_weights`` on the same nodes. ``"jd"`` uses the
Fourier-mode preconditioner on every basis: with ring blocks it ran out of
``jd_outer`` corrections (NaN) on Gauss-Radau-Jacobi at 48x48x16. A restricted
``"dense"`` or ``"eigsh"`` solve keeps the represented space as one block per
radial shell (``assemble.represented_shells``), 4 GB instead of 43 GB on QH at
48x48x16. ``assemble_dense`` with ``ring_nodes`` and a penalty no longer fails
outside ``jit``.
