The Zernike penalty is applied as ``alpha U (U^H u)``, with ``U`` an orthonormal
basis of what the derivative matrices do not represent (``unrepresented_space``).
``I - V V^H`` formed as a matrix has eigenvalues of about -1e-15, which the
whitening turns into negative energies of order ``alpha``: spurious unstable
modes, failed dense Cholesky factorizations, and ``gamma^2`` too large by up to
4e-5 (relative) at ``penalty`` 1e4. ``DiffMat`` stores ``U`` as
``penalty_complement``; ``penalty_projector`` is ``U U^H``.
