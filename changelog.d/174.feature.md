``eigensolver="dense"`` and ``"eigsh"`` build ``A`` from the matrix-free
operator, 64 rows at a time, instead of with ``assemble_dense``, whose
Kronecker-product temporaries are several times the size of ``A``. On the 96x96
DSHAPE tokamak with ``n = 2`` the dense solve peaks at 29.1 GB instead of 68.1 GB
and fits on one 80 GB GPU, and its eigenvalue agrees with the earlier CPU result
to 2e-8.
