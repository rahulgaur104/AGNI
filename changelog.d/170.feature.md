The matrix-free operator on the Zernike basis applies each real derivative
matrix to the real and imaginary parts of a complex vector in one real product,
and applies ``D^T`` by swapping the indices of that product. JAX no longer
promotes the dense derivative matrices to complex or forms their transposes, and
JD on the 96x96 DSHAPE tokamak with ``n = 2`` peaks at 5.6 GB instead of 53.8 GB.
The operator and the ring preconditioner also scatter the two parts separately,
which is about 100 times faster on the GPU than scattering the complex vector.
