``Basis(radial="gauss_radau_jacobi")`` weighted its radial quadrature with the
Jacobi weight ``(1 - x)^alpha (1 + x)^beta`` instead of the plain ``dx`` of the
paper's Eq. 26, so every ``gamma^2`` on that basis was wrong (DSHAPE, ``n = 2``,
96x96: 1.11e-3 instead of 2.91e-4). Its weights are now
``quadrature.interpolatory_weights`` of the same nodes.
