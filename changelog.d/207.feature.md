General flux coordinates: with the two optional fields ``B_sup_theta`` and
``B_sup_zeta`` an ``EquilibriumData`` may be given in any flux coordinates
``(rho, theta, zeta)`` instead of PEST, and the solver replaces the ``iota``
and 1 of the paper's Eq. 20 by ``sqrt(g) B^theta / psi'`` and
``sqrt(g) B^zeta / psi'`` (``agnimhd.general``, ``docs/general_coordinates.md``).
``from_desc``, ``AgniStability``, ``agnimhd.load`` and ``agnimhd solve`` take
``coordinates="native"``: nodes of DESC's own angles, with no Newton solve for
``theta`` and, in ``AgniStability``, DESC's transforms built once. Without the
two fields nothing changes. ``FORMAT_VERSION`` is 4; older files load as before
