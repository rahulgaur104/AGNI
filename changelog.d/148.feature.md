Applies ``penalty`` to the Lobatto and Gauss-Radau-Jacobi bases, on the nodal modes ``D_theta`` maps to zero, and to the null modes of ``D_zeta`` on every basis (``DiffMat.penalty_projector_zeta``): the Nyquist modes and modes above ``mpol`` or ``ntor`` no longer pose as instabilities. The CLI takes it as ``--penalty`` (0 for the plain nodal operator).
