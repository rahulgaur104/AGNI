Adds Bernstein's double-adiabatic (Chew-Goldberger-Low) energy principle for anisotropic pressure. With the optional ``EquilibriumData`` fields ``p_perp``, ``p_par``, ``grad_p_perp``, ``grad_p_par``, ``grad_lnB``, ``T_b``, ``J_sup_rho`` and ``J_sup_theta``, supplied together, every solver assembles it instead of the isotropic energy principle; ``matfree_operator`` also returns ``Ax_full``, the operator on the full ``3 * n_total`` vector.
