Separable bases solve with ``eigsh`` and ``dense`` on the displacements the angular derivative matrices represent (``Basis(restrict=...)``, by Rogerio Jorge), leaving out the Nyquist and truncated modes they map to zero, which on an even-``NFP`` torus can pose as a spurious instability; ``jd``, ``dense_mg``, a parity and the Zernike basis use the penalty. Both are now the default: ``Basis(restrict=None)`` restricts where it can, ``True`` insists, ``False`` always uses the penalty. ``Basis(penalty=None)`` takes its strength from ``AssemblyConfig.axisym``: ``DEFAULT_PENALTY_ALPHA_AXISYM = 1.0`` for a tokamak, ``DEFAULT_PENALTY_ALPHA = 0.05`` in 3D.
