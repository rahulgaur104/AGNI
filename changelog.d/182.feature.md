Free boundary. ``EquilibriumData.phi_matrix`` (the boundary's vacuum response,
``FORMAT_VERSION`` 4) makes every solver free-boundary: ``xi^rho`` is kept on the
outer shell and the vacuum energy is added as in DESC's ``FinitenStability``. ``Basis(...,
free_boundary=True)`` rescales any radial basis so its outer node is ``rho = 1``,
and ``from_desc`` / ``load(...).evaluate`` then compute DESC's
``phi_matrix_pest`` there (``agnimhd.adapters.desc_vacuum``, with the
``phi_*`` options of DESC's ``FinitenStability``); ``agnimhd solve
--free-boundary``. The vacuum response is the solve's toroidal mode family's: for family ``x`` the boundary data and potential are ``exp(i x zeta)`` times NFP-periodic functions, and the boundary integral carries that phase on every field-period copy and polar patch (``phi_matrix(..., n_shift=x)``; complex for ``0 < x < NFP/2``, ``EquilibriumData.phi_n_shift``); on an axisymmetric basis it is the response for ``n_mode_axisym`` (``from_desc(..., n_mode_axisym=)``). With ``stellsym`` (#205) the vacuum source geometry is also evaluated on half
the nodes and mirrored, while the source grid stays the full lattice. The Zernike JD per-mode preconditioner (#172) uses the
same free-boundary mask as the operator. Until DESC's ``Transform(method="pest")``
is merged, ``from_desc`` brings its own forward-only copy for a DESC without it
(``adapters/_desc_pest.py``, temporary) instead of falling back to ``direct1``.
