Free boundary. ``EquilibriumData.phi_matrix`` (the boundary's vacuum response,
``FORMAT_VERSION`` 4) makes every solver free-boundary: ``xi^rho`` is kept on the
outer shell and the vacuum energy is added; ``agnimhd solve --free-boundary``.
The response is one toroidal mode family's (``phi_family``): ``DiffMat.family``
records the family ``D_zeta`` was built for and the assembly refuses a mismatch;
``agnimhd solve`` and ``solve()`` evaluate a DESC file for each family.

Until DESC's ``Transform(method="pest")``is merged, ``from_desc`` brings its own
forward-only copy for a DESC without it (``adapters/_desc_pest.py``, temporary)
instead of falling back to ``direct1``.
