The Zernike mode blocks of the JD preconditioner come from a second operator
whose equilibrium coefficients are averaged over theta and zeta on each radius.
It keeps every ``(m, n)`` mode separate, so one product gives the same column of
every block. Building the blocks takes ``3 n_rho`` matrix-free products instead
of ``3 n_total`` (192 instead of 196,608 at 64x64x16) and 6 to 17 s at every grid
up to 64x64x16. JD still solves the true operator, and on the stellarator family
1 at 16x24x8 it takes as many corrections as with the exact blocks.
