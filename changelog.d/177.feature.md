``dense_mg`` moves its shift halfway to the current ``gamma^2`` estimate after
every successful factorization, and back halfway to the last shift that worked
after a failed one, instead of iterating at a fixed shift. It stops once
``gamma^2`` changes by less than ``SolverConfig.mg_theta_tol`` (new, default 1e-8
relative, 0 turns it off), and all processes share one stop decision, which
roundoff used to split and hang the job. On the 16x16x8 stellarator started at
1e-3, ``gamma^2`` agrees with the one-GPU dense solve to 1.6e-8 or better instead
of 2 to 9 %.
