The ring blocks of the JD preconditioner take each ring's entries of the Zernike
penalty from its (rho, theta) and zeta factors instead of forming the
``n_total x n_total`` penalty matrix as a Kronecker product (34 GB at 64x64x16).
JD on the Patil QH stellarator at 64x64x16 peaks at 6.0 GB instead of 38.7 GB,
with the same ring blocks to rounding.
