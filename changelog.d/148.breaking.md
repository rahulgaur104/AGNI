The penalty is named for what it does, not for the Zernike basis, since every basis uses it now: ``Basis(penalty=...)``, ``DiffMat.penalty_alpha``, ``penalty_projector``, ``penalty_projector_zeta``, ``penalty_rank``, ``penalty_svd_tol``, ``agnimhd.basis.penalty_projector_from_diffmat`` and ``DEFAULT_PENALTY_ALPHA`` replace the ``zernike_penalty`` names, with no aliases.
